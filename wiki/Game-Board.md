The **game board** is a box of puzzles that stands on Home's bookshelf
beside the [daily paper](Daily-Paper). It has the paper's puzzles of the day,
puzzles on a theme you choose (from your own books, the season, a category,
or a theme the AI finds in your books), achievements, and a history of the
puzzles you've finished. Everything is made on the Kindle; it needs no Wi‑Fi.

It's off until you turn it on.

## Turning it on

- In the setup guide: the page **On Home's shelf** asks whether you want the
  daily paper, the game board, or both.
- Later: **Settings → Home and tabs → Game board** (right under Daily paper;
  it says On or Off) **→ Game board on Home's bookshelf**. **Open the game
  board** is there too, for Home's other views (Tiles, List), which have no
  shelf.

The box stands on its side at the start of the first shelf, after the daily
paper when that's on: a dark game box with chequered ends and **GAME BOARD**
along it. Pick it as you pick a book (tap it; with keys, move to it with the
5-way) and it turns to show its lid: a crossword's corner, how many of
today's paper puzzles and of the theme's you've done, and your achievements.
Tap it again (or press) to open it.

## On the board

The board is drawn as a game table, everything on it big enough to tap:

- **Today's paper**: three playing cards, one for each of the [daily
  paper's](Daily-Paper#puzzles) word search, crossword and maze (the board
  has no sudoku), each with a picture of its puzzle. They are the paper's
  own, kept in the same place: a word found on the board is found in the
  paper, and a maze finished in the paper is ticked on the board. A card says
  **Started** once you've begun it, and gets a ✓ stamp when it's done. They
  come with each day's paper (without a paper, from the puzzles' own word
  list).
- **The theme's box lid**: the theme on the board and how many of its
  puzzles are done. Tap it to choose another theme. The **die** beside it
  gives **New puzzles** on the same theme.
- **The theme's cards**: a crossword, a word search, a **word scramble** and
  a maze. They stay until you choose another theme or roll the die; a new
  day doesn't change them.
- **The trophy cabinet**: a cup for every achievement, the ones you've won
  in black. Tap it for the list.
- **The score card**: how many puzzles you've finished, your streak, and the
  last ones finished. Tap it for the whole history.

Touch: tap anything; the round ✕ token closes the board. Keys: the 5-way moves
a frame from card to card, **Press** opens what's framed, **Back** closes; on
a keyboard, **Menu** is the quick panel.

## Themes

| Theme | Its words |
|---|---|
| **From** *your book* | The book you closed last. Names that come up again and again (its people and places) first, then its own longer words. Each clue is a sentence of the book with the word left out. |
| **From another book…** | One of the last six books you've read. |
| **From your books** | Some words of each of the last six books. |
| **This season** | The season's words: winter, spring, summer, autumn, Halloween (October), harvest and Thanksgiving (November), the winter holidays (December). Until you choose a theme, the board has the season's, and it follows the season until you start one of its puzzles. |
| **A category…** | Animals, food and cooking, space, the ocean, weather, the garden, music, sports and games, travel, books and reading. |
| **Another season…** | Any season's words, whatever the month. |
| **Ask the AI for a theme your books share** | Your AI service looks at the titles and authors of the books you've read lately, names a theme they share, and sends fourteen words with clues. Needs an AI key ([Setup page and connections → AI](Setup-Page-and-Connections#ai)). |

A book's words are read when you close it, from the pages you've read (the
last 120 at most) and never further on, so a puzzle gives nothing of the
story away. They're read again after another fifteen pages. EPUBs and
other books with text (not PDFs).

When a theme has too few words for a puzzle, the puzzles' own word list fills
the gaps.

## The word scramble

Six of the theme's words, one at a time, their letters jumbled, with the
word's clue under them. Tap a letter, then another to swap the two; when the
letters spell the word it's ticked and the next one comes. Tap a word in the
list to go to it. **Hint** puts the first wrong letter right.

With keys: **Left** and **Right** choose a letter, **Press** picks it and
**Press** again on another swaps them, **Up** and **Down** go to the word
before or after. On a keyboard, **H** is a hint and **N** starts again.

## Achievements and history

Every puzzle you finish, on the board or in the paper, goes into the history:
when, which puzzle, which theme (or the daily paper), how long from your first
move, and whether you took help (**Check**, or a hint). The last 200 are kept.

Achievements unlock as you go and are shown for a moment when they do: your
first puzzle, ten, fifty and a hundred; one of each kind of puzzle; all three
of a day's paper puzzles on the board; a crossword without **Check**; a puzzle within three
minutes; puzzles from your books (one, then five different books); four
seasons; five categories; an AI theme; three, seven and thirty days running;
a puzzle after midnight, and one before seven in the morning.

## Privacy

The puzzles, your books' words, the history and the achievements stay on the
Kindle. Only **Ask the AI for a theme** sends anything: the titles and
authors of the books you've read lately, to the AI service you set up. The
log notes when a theme is asked for and how many words came back, never the
books or the words.

## What to send if something's wrong

The GZreader log (**Settings → Privacy and diagnostics → Send diagnostic
log**, see [Privacy and diagnostics](Privacy-and-Diagnostics)). Its lines starting **game board:** say when it opened, when a book's
words were read (how many, from which pages, how long it took), when a theme
was chosen and when a puzzle was finished.
