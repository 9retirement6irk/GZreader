Set up a Kindle

Jailbreak it and install GZreader, step by step. This page puts each file on the Kindle at the right moment.

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux or a Chromebook): it writes to the Kindle's drive, and other browsers can't. Open 9retirement6irk.github.io/GZreader/setup there, or follow the steps in the wiki by hand.
Test mode: a pretend Kindle kept in this browser. Nothing is written to a real Kindle.

1 Connect the Kindle

Charge it first. Plug it into the computer with a USB cable: it shows up as a drive. Then choose that drive (the whole drive, not its documents folder). A jailbreak is at your own risk; back up your books first.

No Kindle at hand? Try the page in test mode with a pretend Kindle.

2 Which Kindle

On the Kindle: Menu → Settings (Kindle 2, DX, Keyboard, 4: at the bottom, or press Menu → Device Info), or Settings → Device Options → Device Info. Also on the box. It's only used here, to tell the model; it isn't kept or sent.

3 Step by step

    Everything happens in this browser: the page writes to the Kindle you choose and talks to nothing else, except to download GZreader (from GitHub) and its own files. The jailbreaks are other people's work; the files this page carries and their licences are listed in docs/setup/files.
